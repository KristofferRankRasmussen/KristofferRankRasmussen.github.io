Research

My research focuses on derived categories of the second kind, global Koszul duality, and Hochschild cohomology.

More specifically, I study compactly generated derived categories associated with dg-algebras, with particular emphasis on identifying classes of dg-algebras for which the compact generators admit an explicit description. A better understanding of these generators provides a route towards computing the Hochschild cohomology of the corresponding compactly generated derived categories.

Master's thesis: One-dimensional factorization homology and string-nets

In my master's thesis, I studied factorization homology and string-nets. The main result establishes an equivalence between Kirillov's string-net functor and one-dimensional factorization homology with coefficients in a pivotal fusion category.
